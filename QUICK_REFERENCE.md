## ✏️ Quick Edits

### Change Animation Colors
**File:** `assets/loading-animation.svg`  
**Find:** `<linearGradient id="redGradient">`  
**Change:** `stop-color:#8B0000` and `stop-color:#FF3B3B`

### Update Skills
**File:** `README.md`  
**Find:** `| **Backend** | ... | ████████░░ 80% |`  
**Change:** Percentage and block count
